# Privacy Policy

## Overview

This Privacy Policy explains how Punta, operated by UrbanEase, collects, uses, stores, and protects information when you create an account, post predictions, join Inner Circles, make payments, request payouts, contact support, or otherwise use the app.

Punta is a football predictions and creator community platform. We collect information needed to run accounts, social features, payments, creator payouts, moderation, support, security, analytics, and app reliability.

## Information We Collect

Account information includes your email address, username, display name, profile photo URL when available, profile text, authentication provider, preferred currency, account status, and timestamps.

Activity information includes predictions, odds, fixtures, likes, bookmarks, reposts, shares, follows, followers, Inner Circle memberships or requests, ranking and progression data, leaderboards, and public profile summaries.

Analytics information includes app sessions, engagement, qualified prediction views, creator follows and unfollows, app and device information, and an internal account identifier when you are signed in. Punta does not send your email address or username to Analytics as a user ID.

Payment and earning information may include payment references, payment status, currency, amount, Premium purchases, Inner Circle purchases, creator earnings, withdrawal requests, bank name, bank code, account number, and resolved account name. Punta uses Flutterwave to process payments and verify Nigerian bank account details.

Support and moderation information includes support ticket category, subject, message, sender name, sender email, ticket status, admin notes, user reports, report reasons, moderation decisions, audit logs, and blocked or suspended account records.

## How We Use Information

We use information to create and secure accounts, personalize preferences, show profiles, display predictions and Inner Circles, calculate rankings and progression, manage follows and social interactions, and keep app data synchronized.

We use analytics information to measure app usage and day-one and day-seven retention, understand whether users view predictions and follow creators, improve the product, and diagnose engagement problems.

We use payment and payout information to initialize and verify payments through Flutterwave, unlock Premium or Inner Circle access, calculate creator earnings, review withdrawal requests, resolve bank account names, prevent fraud, and keep financial records.

We use reports, support tickets, audit logs, and account status data to respond to users, investigate abuse, enforce community standards, protect creators and members, and comply with applicable obligations.

## Sharing and Service Providers

Punta shares information with trusted service providers only where needed to operate the app. These include Firebase services for authentication, database storage, app integrity, functions, diagnostics, and Google Analytics for Firebase, and Flutterwave for payments, payment verification, bank lists, account resolution, and payout-related workflows.

Football data and odds providers supply fixtures, results, and odds-related information to Punta. We may also disclose information if required by law, to protect rights and safety, to investigate fraud or abuse, or as part of a business transfer involving Punta or UrbanEase.

## Public Content

Your username, profile information, public predictions, public Inner Circle information, ranking data, and public interactions may be visible to other users. Paid Inner Circle content may be visible to approved members or purchasers based on the circle settings.

Avoid posting private, sensitive, or financial information in predictions, profile text, support screenshots, or other user-visible spaces.

## Storage, Security, and Retention

Punta uses Firebase security rules, authenticated access, server-side Cloud Functions, App Check, and administrative controls to help protect app data. No system can be guaranteed completely secure, but we take reasonable steps to reduce unauthorized access, misuse, and accidental loss.

We keep information for as long as needed to provide Punta, maintain legal or financial records, resolve disputes, enforce terms, prevent fraud, support users, and maintain app integrity.

## Your Choices and Contact

You can update parts of your profile, preferences, and payout details inside the app. Some records, such as payment records, withdrawal records, moderation records, audit logs, and fraud-prevention records, may need to be retained.

For privacy questions, payment concerns, account issues, or support requests, use the in-app Support screen from your signed-in account so Punta can identify the account and respond appropriately.
